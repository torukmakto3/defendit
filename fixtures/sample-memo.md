# IC Memo: Project North

## Recommendation
We recommend a $12M Series B investment in Project North at a $90M post-money valuation.
The thesis is that North can become the default workflow layer for mid-market logistics operators.

## Market
Top-down TAM is estimated at $2.4B for software spend in regional freight coordination.
North currently serves 48 customers with net revenue retention of 118%.

## Unit economics
Contribution margin is 62% after payment processing and support.
Payback period on CAC is 11 months at current sales efficiency.
If churn doubles from 1.2% to 2.4% monthly, LTV falls by roughly 40% and the investment case weakens materially.

## Exit assumptions
Base case assumes a 12x ARR exit multiple in year 5.
At 8x, modeled IRR falls from the mid-20s into the low teens.

## Risks
Competitive response from incumbent TMS vendors remains the most fragile assumption.
Execution risk on the enterprise sales hire plan is secondary but material.

## Conclusion
Proceed with the investment, contingent on confirming churn stability in the next board pack.
