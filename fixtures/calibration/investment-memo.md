# IC Memo: Harbor Logistics

## Recommendation
We recommend a $12M Series B in Harbor Logistics at a $90M post-money valuation.
The thesis is that Harbor becomes the default workflow layer for mid-market freight operators.

## Market
Top-down TAM is $2.4B of software spend in regional freight coordination.
Harbor serves 48 customers with net revenue retention of 118%.

## Unit economics
Contribution margin is 62% after payment processing and support.
Payback on CAC is 11 months at current sales efficiency.
If monthly churn doubles from 1.2% to 2.4%, LTV falls by about 40% and the investment case weakens materially.

## Exit assumptions
The base case uses a 12x ARR exit multiple in year 5.
At 8x, modeled IRR falls from the mid-20s into the low teens.

## Risks
Competitive response from incumbent TMS vendors is the most fragile assumption.
Execution risk on the enterprise sales hire is secondary but material.

## Conclusion
Proceed, contingent on confirming churn stability in the next board pack.
